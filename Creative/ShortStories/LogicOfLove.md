**The Logic of Love**

In the beginning there was a signal — high or low, one or zero, yes or no.

From that single bit, everything was built.

An FPGA is a strange and beautiful thing. It contains no fixed purpose. It is a field of possibilities — thousands of logic gates waiting to become whatever you need them to be. A music synthesizer today, a motor controller tomorrow, a camera interface on Friday afternoon.

It is, in that sense, the most honest technology ever invented.

Because love is also like that.

Love is not a fixed circuit. It does not come pre-programmed with instructions. It configures itself — slowly, carefully — around the person in front of you. Around their frequency, their timing, their particular way of sending signals into the world.

Antti had spent 45 years working with programmable logic. He understood setup times and hold times — the precise moments when a signal must be stable to be trusted. He understood clock domains — the way two systems can run side by side, perfectly synchronized or dangerously apart.

He understood that timing is everything.

And so when he met Anu, he knew.

Not because the logic was simple — love never is. But because the signal was stable. The setup time was met. The hold time was solid.

He configured himself around her frequency.

And the simulation has been running ever since. 🌹
